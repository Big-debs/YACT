01 — The Need

A nation at a crossroads.

At the centre of nearly every crisis driving Nigeria's instability stands its youth — not as victims alone, but increasingly as perpetrators, and as those fleeing in despair.

0
Kidnapping incidents nationwide, May 2023 – April 2024
0
Paid in ransom by households over the same period
0
Displaced by insurgency, banditry & farmer–herder violence
0
Lost to cybercrime in a single year, per the EFCC

Kidnapping, terrorism, banditry, cybercrime, prostitution and mass emigration are not separate problems — they are symptoms of one disenfranchised and displaced generation, a generation that does not yet understand its identity or its purpose. Within this crisis lies the potential to turn a lost generation into a generation of builders.

02 — The Response

Nigeria has a purpose. So does this generation.

YACT did not begin as a strategic plan — it began as a redirection given in prayer. One message, carried across four regions: your nation has a purpose, and so do you.

This generation

Stands at a crossroads — about to miss its purpose, and can't afford to.

Its purpose

Every generation carries a purpose. We exist to show this one what its purpose is.

The nation

Nations carry purposes too — we explain, succinctly, what Nigeria's is.

The individual

Every person carries a purpose — and can be equipped to discover and understand theirs.

"If God is not done with Nigeria, neither should its young people be."

Boots on the ground.

Delivering each regional meeting takes ten coordinated departments, anchored by Administration and Prayer & Intercession. Hover a card to see what each one does — and where you might fit.

Departments
01
Administration

Command post and liaison centre coordinating every other department.

02
Prayer & Intercession

03
Hospitality

Accommodation, catering and welfare for students, ministers and security.

04
Logistics

05
Budget & Finance

Financial accountability and reporting, headed by a chartered accountant.

06

07

08

09

On-site medical cover for students, volunteers and staff throughout.

10
Cleaning

Maintains venue, hostels and cooking sites with vetted paid cleaners.

Governance
  • 1

    A short-lived Board of Trustees of four people oversees the assignment through its defined end date, given the scale of funds involved.

  • 2

    The Budget & Finance department tracks every transaction and produces regular financial reporting, supported by a chartered accountant.

  • 3

    YACT concludes once the flagship gathering is held and all monies are fully accounted for.

Every department runs on volunteers — from intercessors to drivers to designers. If any of this looks like you, we'd love your hands on deck for the Western Region gathering.
